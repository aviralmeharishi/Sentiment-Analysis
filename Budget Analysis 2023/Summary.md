## **🏛️ Comprehensive Analysis of Budget 2023–24)**

---

### 1️⃣ Sector-Wise Allocations & Core Focus Areas

| Sector | Key Announcements & Allocations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🌾 Agriculture & Farmers** | • **Credit Target:** Increased to **₹20 lakh crore** with focused prioritization on animal husbandry, dairy, and fisheries.<br>• **Atmanirbhar Clean Plant Program:** Launched with an outlay of **₹2,200 crore** to boost availability of disease-free planting material.<br>• **Natural Farming:** Setting up **10,000 Bio-Input Resource Centres** over 3 years to support 1 crore farmers in adopting natural farming. |
| **🎓 Education & Skills** | • **Nursing Infrastructure:** Establishment of **157 new nursing colleges** co-located with existing medical colleges.<br>• **National Digital Library:** Set up for children and adolescents to facilitate device-agnostic accessibility across geographies.<br>• **PMKVY 4.0:** Launch of *Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana 4.0* to train youth in new-age tech (coding, AI, robotics, mechatronics). |
| **🏥 Health & Wellness** | • **Sickle Cell Elimination:** Mission launched to eliminate Sickle Cell Anaemia by 2047, targeting universal screening of **7 crore people (ages 0–40)** in tribal areas.<br>• **Pharma R&D:** Promotion of pharmaceutical research and innovation via designated Centres of Excellence.<br>• **Medical Devices:** Introduction of multidisciplinary courses supported across existing institutions. |
| **🚆 Railways & Transport** | • **Railways Outlay:** Highest-ever capital outlay of **₹2.40 lakh crore** (~9 times the outlay in 2013–14).<br>• **Transport Corridors:** 100 critical transport infrastructure projects identified for last/first-mile connectivity with **₹75,000 crore** investment.<br>• **Regional Connectivity:** Revival of **50 additional airports, heliports, and water aerodromes**. |
| **🤖 Technology & AI** | • **AI Centres of Excellence:** Three Centres of Excellence for AI in top educational institutions to foster *"Make AI in India and Make AI Work for India"*.<br>• **National Data Governance Policy:** Launched to unleash research data for academia and startups.<br>• **5G Infrastructure:** 100 labs established across engineering institutions for developing 5G-enabled applications. |
| **🛡️ Defence & Security** | • Implicitly supported via modernization targets and national security frameworks tied into the broader **₹10 lakh crore** capital expenditure framework. |
| **👩 Women / Nari Shakti** | • **Mahila Samman Savings Certificate:** One-time 2-year deposit scheme offering a **7.5% fixed interest rate** (up to ₹2 lakh) for women/girls.<br>• **SHG Scale-up:** Economic empowerment of rural women through Self-Help Groups scaled into large producer enterprises/collectives.<br>• **Artisan Support:** Enhanced credit and market linkages targeting women artisans under *PM VIKAS*. |
| **💼 Youth & Employment** | • **Apprenticeship Scheme:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) rolled out under the National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme to support **47 lakh youth** over 3 years.<br>• **Global Skill Hubs:** Setting up **30 Skill India International Centres** across states.<br>• **Agri-Tech Startups:** *Agriculture Accelerator Fund* launched to encourage rural agri-startups by young entrepreneurs. |
| **🚀 Startups & MSME** | • **Tax Benefits Extension:** Date of incorporation for startup tax holidays extended to **March 31, 2024**; loss carry-forward window increased from 7 to **10 years**.<br>• **Credit Guarantee Revival:** ₹9,000 crore corpus infused into the MSME Credit Guarantee Scheme, unlocking **₹2 lakh crore** in collateral-free credit.<br>• **Vivad se Vishwas I:** Relief to MSMEs by refunding 95% of forfeited security amounts from failed contracts during the COVID period. |
| **🏗️ Infrastructure** | • **Public CapEx:** Capital investment outlay hiked sharply by **33% to ₹10 lakh crore (3.3% of GDP)**.<br>• **Effective CapEx:** Budgeted at **₹13.7 lakh crore (4.5% of GDP)**, including Grants-in-Aid to states.<br>• **State Support:** **₹1.3 lakh crore** allocated as 50-year interest-free loans to state governments to spur regional capital investments. |

### 2️⃣ The 'Rupee Comes From' Analysis (Revenue Profile)

| Revenue Stream | Details & Fiscal Estimates (RE 2022–23 / BE 2023–24) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Receipts (Excluding Borrowings)** | • **Revised Estimate (2022–23):** ₹24.3 lakh crore<br>• **Budget Estimate (2023–24):** ₹27.2 lakh crore |
| **Net Tax Receipts** | • **Revised Estimate (2022–23):** ₹20.9 lakh crore<br>• **Budget Estimate (2023–24):** ₹23.3 lakh crore |
| **Gross & Net Market Borrowings** | • **Gross Market Borrowings:** ₹15.4 lakh crore (BE 2023–24)<br>• **Net Market Borrowings (Dated Securities):** ₹11.8 lakh crore (BE 2023–24) |
| **Additional Financing Channels** | Balance financing sourced via small savings schemes, provident funds, and short-term debt instruments. |
| **Tax Proposals & Fiscal Impact** | • Net revenue forgone due to tax rationalization: **~₹35,000 crore annually**<br>• *Direct Taxes:* ₹37,000 crore forgone (relief to middle class & revisions in New Tax Regime)<br>• *Indirect Taxes:* ₹1,000 crore forgone; Additional mobilization: ₹3,000 crore |

### 3️⃣ The 'Rupee Goes To' Analysis (Expenditure Profile)

| Expenditure Head | Details & Allocations (RE 2022–23 / BE 2023–24) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Central Expenditure** | • **Revised Estimate (2022–23):** ₹41.9 lakh crore<br>• **Budget Estimate (2023–24):** **₹45.0 lakh crore** |
| **Capital Expenditure (CapEx)** | • **Revised Estimate (2022–23):** ₹7.3 lakh crore<br>• **Budget Estimate (2023–24):** **₹10.0 lakh crore** *(+33% YoY increase, 3.3% of GDP)* |
| **Effective Capital Expenditure** | Budgeted at **₹13.7 lakh crore (4.5% of GDP)**, accounting for capital asset creation grants provided to states. |
| **Subnational Capital Assistance** | **₹1.3 lakh crore** provided via 50-year interest-free loans for state infrastructure projects. |
| **Major Strategic Scheme Allocations** | • **PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (Free Foodgrains):** ~₹2,00,000 crore<br>• **Railways Capital Outlay:** ₹2,40,000 crore<br>• **PM Awas Yojana (Affordable Housing):** Enhanced by 66% to > ₹79,000 crore<br>• **Critical Transport Infrastructure (100 Projects):** ₹75,000 crore<br>• **Energy Transition & Net-Zero (MoPNG):** ₹35,000 crore<br>• **Ladakh Renewable Energy Transmission Line:** ₹20,700 crore (Central support: ₹8,300 crore)<br>• **National Green Hydrogen Mission:** ₹19,700 crore<br>• **Pradhan Mantri PVTG Development Mission:** ₹15,000 crore over 3 years<br>• **Urban Infrastructure Development Fund (UIDF):** ₹10,000 crore per annum<br>• **MSME Credit Guarantee Corpus:** ₹9,000 crore<br>• **PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (Sub-scheme):** ₹6,000 crore<br>• **Upper Bhadra Project (Karnataka):** ₹5,300 crore central grant<br>• **Atmanirbhar Clean Plant Program:** ₹2,200 crore |
| **Fiscal Deficit Consolidation Path** | • **RE (2022–23):** 6.4% of GDP<br>• **BE (2023–24):** 5.9% of GDP<br>• **Target Glide Path:** Committed to lowering fiscal deficit **below 4.5% of GDP by FY 2025–26** |
