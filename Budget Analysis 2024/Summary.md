# **Summary of Budget**
---
## 1. Sector-Wise Allocations & Focus

**Agriculture & Farmers**
* Provision of ₹1.52 lakh crore for agriculture and allied sectors.
* 109 high-yielding, climate-resilient crop varieties to be released; 1 crore farmers initiated into natural farming.
* Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) to cover 6 crore farmers and their lands in 3 years.

**Education & Skills**
* Provision of ₹1.48 lakh crore for education, employment, and skilling.
* Upgradation of 1,000 Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) in hub-and-spoke arrangements.
* Financial support for domestic higher education loans up to ₹10 lakh with 3% interest subvention e-vouchers.

**Health & Wellness**
* Full exemption of basic customs duties on 3 additional cancer medicines.
* Customs duty rationalization on X-ray tubes and flat panel detectors to foster domestic capacity.
* Setting up of new medical colleges and sports infrastructure in Bihar.

**Railways & Transport**
* Development of major road expressways and spurs in Bihar (Patna-Purnea, Buxar-Bhagalpur, Bodhgaya-Darbhanga) at ₹26,000 crore.
* Infrastructure funding for industrial nodes along Vishakhapatnam-Chennai and Hyderabad-Bengaluru corridors (including railways).
* Reforms in ownership, leasing, and flagging for the Indian shipping and domestic cruise tourism industry.

**Technology & AI**
* Development of population-scale Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) applications for credit, e-commerce, health, law, and MSMEs.
* Setting up an Integrated Technology Platform for the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) ecosystem.
* Basic customs duty on PCBA of specified telecom equipment increased from 10% to 15% to boost domestic manufacturing.

**Defence & Security**
* Created new tariff lines for defence products to align with World Customs Organization (WCO) standards.
* Duty exemptions on components/consumables for manufacturing vessels and aircraft for defence and coast guard.
* Customs exemptions extended to technical documentation and spare parts for warship construction.

**Women / Nari Shakti**
* Allocation of over ₹3 lakh crore for schemes benefitting women and girls.
* Setting up of working women hostels and crèches in collaboration with industry.
* Encouraging states to lower property stamp duties for purchases made by women.

**Youth & Employment**
* Prime Minister’s package of 5 schemes for 4.1 crore youth over 5 years with a central outlay of ₹2 lakh crore.
* Implementation of 3 Employment Linked Incentive schemes based on EPFO enrolment.
* Internship scheme in 500 top companies for 1 crore youth over 5 years with a ₹5,000 monthly allowance.

**Startups & MSME**
* Total abolition of Angel Tax for all classes of investors to foster the startup ecosystem.
* Credit guarantee scheme providing up to ₹100 crore cover for MSMEs purchasing machinery/equipment.
* Enhancement of Mudra loan limit to ₹20 lakh under the 'Tarun' category for past successful borrowers.

**Infrastructure**
* Capital expenditure provision of ₹11,11,111 crore (3.4% of GDP).
* Provision of ₹1.5 lakh crore in long-term interest-free loans to states for capital investment.
* Development of 12 industrial parks and plug-and-play industrial parks in/near 100 cities.

## 2. The 'Rupee Comes From' Analysis (Revenue)

| Revenue Source | Details / Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Non-Borrowing Receipts** | Estimated at ₹32.07 lakh crore for FY 2024-25. |
| **Net Tax Receipts** | Estimated at ₹25.83 lakh crore. |
| **Gross & Net Market Borrowings** | Gross market borrowings estimated at ₹14.01 lakh crore; Net market borrowings at ₹11.63 lakh crore. |
| **Direct Taxes (Personal & Corporate)** | Over 58% of corporate tax collected via simplified regime (FY23); Corporate tax rate on foreign companies reduced from 40% to 35%. Slabs rationalized under new personal tax regime. |
| **Indirect Taxes (GST & Customs)** | GST simplified and expanded; Customs duties rationalized (reduced on gold, silver to 6%, platinum to 6.4%, critical minerals, and mobile devices; increased on ammonium nitrate and PVC flex banners). |
| **Capital Gains & Financial Transaction Taxes** | Short-term capital gains on specified financial assets raised to 20%; Long-term capital gains tax set at 12.5%; Securities Transaction Tax (STT) on futures raised to 0.02% and options to 0.1%. Share buy-back income taxed as dividend. |
