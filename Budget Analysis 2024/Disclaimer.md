# **⚠️ Disclaimer & Context**

### 📌 Interim Budget vs. Full Budget
* **Interim Budget (February 2024):** A transitional *Vote on Account* passed prior to the 2024 General Elections to authorize essential government expenditures without introducing major taxation changes.
* **Full Union Budget (July 2024):** The comprehensive *Annual Financial Statement* presented by the newly elected government, outlining key policy reforms, revised tax structures, and sector-wise budgetary outlays for Fiscal Year 2024–25.
