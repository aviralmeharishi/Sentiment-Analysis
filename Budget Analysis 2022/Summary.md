


# **🏛️ Comprehensive Economic Analysis (Budget 2022–23)**

---

### 1️⃣ Sector-Wise Allocations & Focus Areas

| Sector | Key Announcements & Allocations |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🌾 Agriculture & Farmers** | • **Direct MSP Payout:** ₹2.37 lakh crore direct payment of MSP value transferred to 163 lakh farmers for wheat and paddy procurement.<br>• **Natural Farming:** Promotion of chemical-free natural farming along the river Ganga corridor and support for millet products (*International Year of Millets 2023*).<br>• **Hi-Tech Agri Services:** PPP-mode scheme launched for digital and hi-tech services, including **'Kisan Drones'** for crop assessment, digitization of land records, and spraying of nutrients.<br>• **Ken-Betwa Link Project:** Estimated cost of ₹44,605 crore, with **₹1,400 crore allocated in 2022–23**, providing irrigation benefits to 9.08 lakh hectares of farmland. |
| **🎓 Education & Skills** | • **PM eVIDYA Expansion:** ‘One Class-One TV Channel’ program expanded from 12 to **200 TV channels** to provide supplementary education in regional languages for classes 1–12.<br>• **Virtual & Skilling Labs:** 750 virtual labs established in science/maths and 75 skilling e-labs for simulated vocational learning.<br>• **DESH-Stack e-Portal:** Launched for online skilling, reskilling, and upskilling, providing API-based credentials and citizen job discovery.<br>• **Digital University:** Established to provide world-class, personalized, and device-agnostic higher education across India. |
| **🏥 Health & Wellness** | • **National Digital Health Ecosystem:** Open platform rolled out under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (digital registries, unique health identities, and consent frameworks).<br>• **National Tele Mental Health Programme:** Launched with a network of **23 tele-mental health centres of excellence**, anchored by NIMHANS as the nodal agency. |
| **🚆 Railways & Transport** | • **PM GatiShakti Masterplan:** Driven by **7 Engines of Growth** (*Roads, Railways, Airports, Ports, Mass Transport, Waterways, and Logistics Infrastructure*).<br>• **National Highways Expansion:** Network to be expanded by **25,000 km in 2022–23**, mobilizing ₹20,000 crore via innovative financing.<br>• **Vande Bharat & Logistics:** Development of **400 new-generation Vande Bharat Trains** and **100 PM GatiShakti Cargo Terminals** over the next 3 years.<br>• **Kavach Safety System:** 2,000 km of railway track network brought under the indigenous 'Kavach' anti-collision technology. |
| **🤖 Technology & AI** | • **5G Rollout:** Spectrum auctions conducted in 2022 to facilitate commercial 5G mobile services rollout within 2022–23.<br>• **Design-Led Manufacturing:** Dedicated PLI scheme introduced to build a robust domestic 5G ecosystem.<br>• **BharatNet via PPP:** Contracts awarded for laying optical fibre cables in all villages through public-private partnerships, targeting 2025 completion.<br>• **Digital Rupee (CBDC):** Introduction of the Central Bank Digital Currency by the Reserve Bank of India using blockchain technology. |
| **🛡️ Defence & Security** | • **Domestic Procurement:** **68% of the capital procurement budget** earmarked for the domestic defence industry in 2022–23 (up from 58% in 2021–22).<br>• **Defence R&D Opening:** 25% of the defence R&D budget earmarked for private industry, startups, and academia. |
| **👩 Women / Nari Shakti** | • **Integrated MWCD Missions:** Comprehensive revamp launching *Mission Shakti*, *Mission Vatsalya*, and *Saksham Anganwadi & Poshan 2.0*.<br>• **Saksham Anganwadi:** Upgradation of **2 lakh anganwadis** with modernized infrastructure, audio-visual aids, and clean energy. |
| **💼 Youth & Employment** | • **PLI Employment Engine:** Productivity Linked Incentive (PLI) across 14 sectors with the potential to create **60 lakh new jobs** and ₹30 lakh crore in additional production over 5 years.<br>• **AVGC Promotion Task Force:** Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, and Comic task force established to unlock domestic potential and employ youth. |
| **🚀 Startups & MSME** | • **ECLGS Extension:** Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme extended up to March 2023, expanding guarantee cover by ₹50,000 crore to **₹5 lakh crore** (focused on hospitality & tourism).<br>• **CGTMSE Revamp:** Infusion of funds to facilitate **₹2 lakh crore additional collateral-free credit** for micro & small enterprises.<br>• **RAMP Programme:** *Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance* launched with a ₹6,000 crore outlay over 5 years.<br>• **Startup Tax Holidays:** 3-year consecutive tax exemption window extended by one additional year up to March 31, 2023. |
| **🏗️ Infrastructure** | • **Public CapEx Surge:** Capital outlay stepped up sharply by **35.4% to ₹7.50 lakh crore** in 2022–23 (2.9% of GDP).<br>• **Effective CapEx:** Estimated at **₹10.68 lakh crore (4.1% of GDP)** including grants-in-aid to states.<br>• **Har Ghar, Nal Se Jal:** **₹60,000 crore** allocated to provide tap water connectivity to 3.8 crore households.<br>• **PM Awas Yojana:** **₹48,000 crore** allocated to complete 80 lakh affordable houses.<br>• **PM-DevINE:** Initial allocation of ₹1,500 crore for regional infrastructure and livelihood projects in the North-East. |

### 2️⃣ The 'Rupee Comes From' Analysis (Revenue Profile)

| Revenue Source / Statutory Head | Details & Policy Provisions (BE 2022–23) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Non-Borrowing Receipts** | **₹22.84 lakh crore** (Estimated) |
| **Fiscal Deficit Target** | Budgeted at **6.4% of GDP** (Glide path aimed below 4.5% by FY 2025–26) |
| **Direct Taxes — Structural & Policy Reforms** | • **Updated Return (ITR-U):** Provision allowing taxpayers to file an updated return within **2 years** from the end of the relevant assessment year on payment of additional tax.<br>• **Virtual Digital Assets (Crypto / NFTs):** Flat **30% tax** on transfer of virtual digital assets (no deduction except cost of acquisition; no loss set-off against other income; **1% TDS** on transactions above threshold).<br>• **Cooperative Societies Relief:** Alternate Minimum Tax (AMT) reduced from 18.5% to **15%**; Surcharge reduced from 12% to **7%** for income between ₹1 Cr and ₹10 Cr.<br>• **State Employees NPS Parity:** Tax deduction on employer’s NPS contribution raised from 10% to **14%** for State Government staff (at par with Central staff).<br>• **Manufacturing Concessional Tax (Sec 115BAB):** Last date of commencement extended by 1 year to March 31, 2024.<br>• **LTCG Surcharge Cap:** Surcharge on long-term capital gains on transfer of any asset class capped at **15%**.<br>• **Anti-Avoidance Clarifications:** Health & Education Cess clarified as strictly *non-deductible* as business expenditure; no loss set-off against undisclosed search income. |
| **Indirect Taxes (GST & Customs Reforms)** | • **Customs Phasing & Make-in-India:** Concessional tariffs on capital goods and project imports gradually phased out, applying a moderate standard tariff of **7.5%**.<br>• **Exemption Pruning:** Over **350 customs duty exemptions phased out** (chemicals, fabrics, medical equipment where domestic capacity exists).<br>• **Gems & Jewellery:** Customs duty on cut and polished diamonds/gemstones reduced to **5%** (nil on sawn diamonds); duty on imitation jewellery set to at least ₹400/kg.<br>• **Electronics & Solar:** Duty rationalized on hearables, wearables, smart meters, and solar cell manufacturing components.<br>• **SEZ Customs IT Integration:** Customs administration of SEZs shifted to a fully paperless, IT-driven platform by September 30, 2022. |
| **Excise Duty Provisions** | • **Differential Fuel Duty:** Additional excise duty of **₹2 per litre** levied on unblended fuel from October 1, 2022, to incentivize ethanol blending. |
