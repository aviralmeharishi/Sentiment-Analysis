# **Summary**

### 1. Sector-Wise Strategic Focus & Policy Announcements

| Sector | Key Strategic Interventions & Policy Commitments |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agriculture & Farmers** | • **Nano-DAP Rollout:** Expanding Nano-DAP fertilizer applications across all distinct agro-climatic zones.<br>• **Atmanirbhar Oilseeds Abhiyan:** Formulating a dedicated self-reliance roadmap covering mustard, groundnut, sesame, soybean, and sunflower.<br>• **Allied Sectors:** Accelerating the *PM Matsya Sampada Yojana* for aquaculture and rolling out an integrated development framework for dairy farmers. |
| **Education & Skills** | • **Institutional Expansion:** Establishing major premier institutions across the decade (7 IITs, 16 IIITs, 7 IIMs, 15 AIIMS, and 390 universities).<br>• **Vocational Skilling:** *Skill India Mission* reports training 1.4 crore youth, reskilling 54 lakh candidates, and creating 3,000 modern ITIs.<br>• **School Education:** Scaling *PM SHRI* schools to deliver foundational pedagogy aligned with NEP 2020. |
| **Health & Wellness** | • **Medical Education:** Setting up a dedicated high-level committee to leverage existing hospital infrastructure for new medical colleges.<br>• **Preventive Oncology:** Promoting a national vaccination campaign for adolescent girls (aged 9–14) against cervical cancer.<br>• **Social Security:** Broadening *Ayushman Bharat* healthcare coverage to include all frontline ASHA, Anganwadi workers, and helpers. |
| **Railways & Transport** | • **Gati Shakti Corridors:** Executing three dedicated economic rail corridors: energy/mineral/cement, port connectivity, and high-traffic density routes.<br>• **Fleet Modernization:** Upgrading 40,000 conventional rail bogies to high-speed *Vande Bharat* safety and comfort standards.<br>• **Urban Transit:** Rapidly expanding *Metro Rail* and *NaMo Bharat* regional rapid transit networks; national airport tally doubled to 149. |
| **Technology & Innovation** | • **Sunrise Innovation Corpus:** Establishing a landmark **₹1 lakh crore** pool offering 50-year interest-free loans for long-term private R&D in deep-tech and emerging domains.<br>• **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI):** Formally institutionalizing DPI as a modern 'factor of production' to accelerate formalization of the economy. |
| **Defence & Security** | • **Deep-Tech for Defence:** Launching a dedicated technology mission to indigenize critical military hardware and deep-tech platforms.<br>• **Strategic Modernization:** Reinforcing border infrastructure and self-reliance (*Aatmanirbharta*) under the long-term *Amrit Kaal* doctrine. |
| **Women Empowerment (*Nari Shakti*)** | • **Lakhpati Didi:** Upwardly revising the empowerment target from 2 crore to **3 crore rural women** via 83 lakh self-help groups (SHGs).<br>• **Asset Ownership:** Mandating over 70% of *PM Awas Yojana (Gramin)* rural houses to women as sole or joint proprietors.<br>• **STEM Inclusivity:** Noting women's enrollment in tertiary STEM programs at 43%, among the highest globally. |
| **Youth & Employment** | • **Entrepreneurial Credit:** *PM Mudra Yojana* crosses 43 crore sanctions totaling **₹22.5 lakh crore** for micro-enterprises.<br>• **Ecosystem Incentives:** Extending catalytic institutional credit via *Fund of Funds*, *Startup India*, and targeted credit guarantee mechanisms.<br>• **Sports Infrastructure:** Sustained talent investments reflecting in record Asian Games medal hauls and rising global chess titles. |
| **Startups & MSMEs** | • **Global Competitiveness:** Institutionalizing policy support for timely credit flow, technology adoption, and international regulatory readiness.<br>• **Tax Holiday Extension:** Prolonging operational tax holidays for eligible startups, sovereign wealth funds, and IFSC GIFT City units until March 31, 2025. |
| **Infrastructure & Energy** | • **Capital Expenditure Outlay:** Stepping up sovereign capex by 11.1% to a historic **₹11,11,111 crore** (3.4% of projected GDP).<br>• **PM Surya Ghar (Rooftop Solar):** Powering 1 crore households with up to 300 units of free monthly electricity.<br>• **Clean Transition:** Providing viability gap funding (VGF) for offshore wind and targeting 100 MT coal gasification/liquefaction capacity by 2030. |
